# Bays has moved

Bays now ships as **[Bays (SideTabs)](https://marketplace.visualstudio.com/items?itemName=Lovervoid.sidetabs)** (`Lovervoid.sidetabs`).

This last release of `Lovervoid.bays` does three things:

- installs `Lovervoid.sidetabs` for you;
- hands over your group names, colours, locks and view preferences, once per workspace;
- adds nothing of its own, so the two never clash.

Your settings keep working as they are: they still use the `bays.` prefix.

Once the new extension shows your groups, you can uninstall this one.
